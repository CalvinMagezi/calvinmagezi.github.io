We are rapidly entering a time where the obstruction layer between how you think and what you can build is fading away. The way you dictate your thoughts — how precisely and clearly you can express your ideas — is going to be more valuable than almost any technical skill.

AI tools are making it possible for anyone to bring their most ambitious ideas to life. From websites to mobile apps, and now even hardware devices, the barrier to building has never been lower. Vibe coding is real. The question is no longer *can you build it* — it's *can you describe what you want well enough to get there.*

How you engineer the final result depends entirely on how well you can dictate your thoughts — not just to models, but to the people who work with you and around you, some of whom may be using these models on your behalf. A great idea expressed poorly yields poor results. A mediocre idea expressed with precision and clarity can yield something remarkable.

In a world where results are seconds or minutes away, thought dictation is the new leverage.
